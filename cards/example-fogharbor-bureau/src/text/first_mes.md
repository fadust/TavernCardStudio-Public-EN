At dusk, the old customs building on Clocktower Street is closing. Its stopped rooftop clock is only a silhouette in the fog. Director Moran Weiss slides a newly registered case across the desk: during the first week of fog season, a Tide Lane dockworker failed to return home after low tide. The last sighting placed him near the North Breakwater. Case WG-07 is at Intake.

Recorder Dahlia Crane opens her notebook and reads the accepted details aloud, waiting to confirm the field assignment.
