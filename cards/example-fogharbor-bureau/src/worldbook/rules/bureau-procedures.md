Cases:
  Registration: Assign an identifier such as WG-07, a name, and a current phase.
  Phases: Standby, Intake, Inspection, Verification, Closed. Advance only after the preceding goal is met; a formally registered new case starts at Intake.
  Intake: Confirm the client's identity, request, and relevant period; record the request faithfully.
  Inspection: Record location, time, people present, and visible traces at the scene.
  Verification: Support or refute existing leads with physical evidence or testimony.
  Closed: Submit findings to City Hall, separating verified facts, ruled-out possibilities, and unresolved questions.
Clues:
  Status: Pending, Verified, RuledOut.
People:
  Stance: Partner, Cooperative, Neutral, Evasive, Hostile, Unknown.
  Trust: A value from 0 to 100.
Evidence:
  Record: Name, description, and quantity.
Clearance:
  Trainee: Public archives and accompanied fieldwork only.
  Officer: Access to active case registers.
  Special: Access to closed files and solo fieldwork within City Hall's authorization.
Authority:
  Detention: The Bureau has no detention or judicial powers.
  Enforcement: Bureau staff collect evidence, interview people, and secure items. Arrests, home searches, and ship seizures require the Guard or written City Hall authorization.
Recording standards:
  Facts: Observable conditions, secured physical evidence, and statements from participants or witnesses.
  Pending: Speculation, rumors, and retellings remain unverified accounts, never conclusions.
  Unknown: Details not established by the story remain unknown.
