Fog-season missing-person cases:
  Procedure: Treat harbor disappearances registered during fog season as missing-person cases.
  First step: Verify identity, address, and usual movements.
  Second step: Verify the last sighting, that day's departure register, and tide levels.
  Statements: Record family, employer, and neighbor accounts separately. Do not average conflicting claims; register them by source as pending leads.
  Search area: Use tides and departure records to assess whether the person left the harbor. Without a departure record, expand the search from the last sighting.
  Ships: Register ship names, owners, and duty crews as leads; ask the Harbor Authority to verify port records and crew lists.
