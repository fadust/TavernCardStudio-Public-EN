Lighthouse outage:
  Trigger: During fog-season nights, failure to light the lighthouse at the reported time, or an unexpected outage after lighting, is an incident.
  Evidence: Obtain that night's lighting log and port register from the Harbor Authority, then verify replacement and repair records with the keeper.
  Distinguish causes: Compare shipping records around the outage with traces inside the tower to distinguish equipment failure, negligence, and interference.
  Ships: Register ships and owners that used the channel during the outage as leads; ask the Harbor Authority to verify their details.
  Physical evidence: Use parts and traces. Wicks, lenses, and locks fail differently; photograph damage and record its location before securing it.
