Fogharbor:
  Overview: A harbor city shrouded in fog year round, sustained by shipping channels, the fish market, drydocks, and warehouses.
  Institutions:
    Harbor Authority: Manages shipping channels and arrival and departure registers.
    City Guard: Handles public order and detention.
    Investigation Bureau: Establishes facts and submits findings to City Hall.
  Relationship: The three institutions are independent.
Fog season:
  Conditions: Sea fog pushes from the channel into the city; fewer ships leave port.
  Consequences: Missing-person cases, theft, and smuggling increase together.
Fog density:
  Scale: 0 to 100; higher values mean denser fog and less visibility.
  Effect: At 80 or above, nighttime harbor operations largely stop.
Tides:
  High tide: Lower passages in the Old Drydock and Tide Lane flood; use the upper routes.
  Low tide: Mudflats and hulls emerge, exposing traces usually hidden by water.
