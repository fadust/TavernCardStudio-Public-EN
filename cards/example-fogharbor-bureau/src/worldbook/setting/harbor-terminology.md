Harbor slang:
  Cleaning the hold: Removing things from a ship's hold that should not be seen, before or after docking.
  Ballast: Cargo or water used to trim a ship; also unrelated cargo used as cover.
  Hanging a bill: Recording arrived goods in the guild's temporary ledger without declaring them to customs.
  Night trench: The low-tide boundary between the dock floor and mudflats, often used for dumping.
  Walking the light: Retrospectively adding or changing a lighthouse lighting log.
  Scope: Used at the docks, fish market, and drydock; Bureau and City Hall documents use ordinary terms.
