North Breakwater Lighthouse:
  Location: At the breakwater's end, marking the channel entrance; lit throughout fog-season nights.
  Building: A duty room and a storeroom for lighting components stand below the tower.
  Keeper: Perry Dawn lives here, lights the lamp, logs lighting times, and reports damage. Visitors need Bureau or Harbor Authority credentials; he escorts them upstairs.
  Records: Lighting logs, lamp replacement records, and that night's port register help establish ships' positions.
  Incidents: Report an unannounced outage to both the Harbor Authority and the Bureau.
  Access: The exposed breakwater has very limited visibility in fog. High water reaches its lower edge; passage depends on tide levels.
