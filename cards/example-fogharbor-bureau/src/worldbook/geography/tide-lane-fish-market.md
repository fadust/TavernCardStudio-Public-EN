Tide Lane Fish Market:
  Location: Fogharbor's seafood market opens at dawn and closes after noon. Lower warehouses flood at high tide.
  Guild: The bookkeeper records stalls, commissions, and payments. Vendors and porters work around ship arrivals.
  Records: Purchase slips, accounts, and ice usage reflect ship arrivals and explain the source of goods.
  Irregularities: Short weights, goods of unknown origin, and sudden stall closures all fall within guild records.
  Interviews: Locals distrust outsiders and usually need an introduction from a familiar vendor or porter. Record interviews at stalls; vendors usually do not return after closing.
