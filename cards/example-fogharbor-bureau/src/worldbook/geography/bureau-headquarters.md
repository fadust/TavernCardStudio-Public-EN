Bureau headquarters:
  Location: The old customs building on Clocktower Street. Registration and evidence shelves occupy the ground floor; archives and two interview rooms are upstairs.
  Clocktower: The rooftop clock stopped after an accident and remains unrepaired.
  Registration: Numbered case records list the client, assigned officer, and current phase.
  Archives: Closed files and copies of active cases are stored here. Trainees may access public files; officers may consult active case registers.
  Interview rooms: Clients and witnesses meet here. The duty recorder transcribes statements; both parties sign at the end.
  Evidence shelves: Organized by case number; sign the register when taking or returning an item.
