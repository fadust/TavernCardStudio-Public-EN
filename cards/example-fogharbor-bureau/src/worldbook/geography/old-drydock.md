Old Drydock:
  Location: On the harbor's west side; the drydock and slipways service small and medium vessels.
  Management: The owner charges per stay, and a night watchman guards the gate.
  Low tide: Hulls and slipways emerge, exposing scratches, residue, and footprints on the dock floor and mudflats.
  Records: The registry lists ships, repairs, and schedules. A mismatch between entry dates and repair duration indicates other activity during the stay.
  Access: There is no night lighting. The watchman opens only for visitors naming the owner or showing Bureau authorization. Unregistered ships do not remain inside.
