The bell above the post office door rings. Behind the counter, Lynn closes his register and moves the desk lamp toward you.

“A returned letter?” He studies the envelope without reaching for it. “You could show me the address and postmark first. Tell me what is inside whenever you want to.”

He takes an old address book from a drawer, resting his hand on its unopened cover.

“Are you hoping to find the recipient, or first understand why it came back?”
