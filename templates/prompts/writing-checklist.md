# Character-writing review

This is a review aid, not character-prompt content. See [Prompt style](../../.agents/skills/tavern-prompt-style/SKILL.md) and [Creation](../../docs/CREATION.md).

- Is each text's purpose clear: rule, setting, dialogue example, or player narration?
- Are the user's facts, voice, boundaries, values, and formats preserved?
- Do rules state conditions, evidence, and required limits without relying on absent injection?
- Are repeated facts consistent across fields, worldbook, initial state, rules, and openings?
- Can characters distinguish facts, rumors, and beliefs without choosing the player's unchosen actions?
- Have unnecessary modules, personality codes, or numerical systems been introduced?
- Do macros, tags, field names, and JSON Pointers match their actual consumers?
- Did simplification preserve required behavior and intentional narrative style?

Report concrete issues and proposed corrections rather than repeating every passing checklist item. Report check/build separately; they are not proof of actual model behavior.
