# Start with a conversation

Open the workspace in your agent and say what you want. No fixed startup prompt is required. Examples:

- “Install the environment this workspace needs and confirm that the tool starts.”
- “Import this card, inspect its structure, and help me revise it.”
- “Organize this card's worldbook while preserving its setting.”
- “I want to create an inn-management card. Let's discuss ideas first.”

[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the relevant skills supply the workflow. Requirements can develop during the conversation. If the agent has not read the workspace guidance, say “Read AGENTS.md first.”
